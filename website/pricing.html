02 / Terms

Questions before deployment.

When is a commercial license needed?
It is needed when you want closed-source or hosted use without meeting AGPL-3.0 obligations, when you need a Pro capability, or when your organization does not permit AGPL dependencies.
How is a license verified?
A signed key is supplied through LUMMENNA_LICENSE_KEY and verified locally. Validation does not require a license-server request.
What are the current prices and limits?
Price, term, seats, support response, and other commercial terms are specified in the signed order form. Contact licensing@quantumindssi.com for a current quote.
Are roadmap capabilities included?
No. Planned features are not current capabilities and are not part of a license grant until they ship and enter the enforceable feature registry.
Where are the controlling terms?
A commercial license summary explaining the model is sent on request from licensing@quantumindssi.com. A signed order form or master agreement contains the binding commercial terms.
